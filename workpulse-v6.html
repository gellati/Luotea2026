The Core Insight
WHY USERS DON'T KNOW WHAT THEY KNOW

Users have already voted with their feet — 51.5% utilisation in named 84-zones vs 24.8% in generic desks. The preference is real and consistent.


But newcomers have no map. They default to the generic grid and get a worse experience — not because the building is bad, but because the knowledge isn't shared.


The cleaning contract makes this worse: generic desks are cleaned at the same frequency as hot zones regardless of actual use. Resources follow the contract, not the demand.
